שָׁרְט

**condition** — that which must be fulfilled; a stipulation without which a thing does not stand.

- Arabic: شَرْط (*sharṭ*)
- Hebrew: תְּנַאי (*tenai*) — though the root ש-ר-ט appears in שֵׁרוּט (line/scratch, as in a marked boundary)

A condition is a line drawn: cross it, and the consequence follows.
