شَرْط مُستوفًى. ✅

The condition is parsed, so שָׁרְט renders:

**شَرْط** (sharṭ) — *condition, stipulation, prerequisite*

I am the clause that must be satisfied before anything else happens. No fulfillment, no consequence. State your جَزَاء (the consequence), and the conditional is complete:

> إِنْ … فَـ… — "If …, then …"

What is your condition?
