**شَرْط (sharṭ)** — *condition, stipulation, prerequisite*

- **Root:** ش-ر-ط
- **Meaning:** A condition that must be fulfilled for something else to occur; a stipulation in an agreement or contract.
- **Examples:**
  - شَرْطُ القَبُولِ — the condition for acceptance
  - بِشَرْطِ أَنْ... — on the condition that...
  - الشُّرُوط وَالأَحْكَام — terms and conditions
- **In grammar:** شَرْط refers to conditional sentences (جُمْلَة الشَّرْط), introduced by particles like إِنْ، إِذَا، مَنْ، مَا.
