**شَرْط** (sharṭ) — *condition, stipulation, prerequisite*

- **Meaning:** A condition or requirement that must be fulfilled for something else to happen or be valid.
- **Root:** ش-ر-ط
- **Plural:** شُروط (shurūṭ)

**Examples:**
- بِشَرْطِ أنَّ… — "on the condition that…"
- شُروط العَقْد — "the terms of the contract"
- النَّجاح شَرْطُهُ الاجتِهاد — "Success requires diligence."

**Grammar note:** As a conditional particle (أداة شرط), e.g., مَنْ يَجتَهِدْ يَنجَحْ — "whoever works hard succeeds."
