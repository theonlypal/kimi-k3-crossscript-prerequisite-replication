☀️ **شَمْس (shams)** — *the sun*

**Root:** ش-م-س
**Gender:** Feminine (اسم مؤنث)

The celestial body at the center of our solar system; also used for sunlight and warmth.

**Examples:**
- طَلَعَتِ الشَّمْسُ — The sun rose.
- أَشِعَّةُ الشَّمْسِ — The sun's rays.

**In the Qur'an:** وَالشَّمْسِ وَضُحَاهَا — "By the sun and its morning brightness" (Surah Ash-Shams, 91:1)

**Note:** When the definite article attaches, the ش assimilates: الشَّمْس is pronounced *ash-shams* (a "sun letter" — حرف شمسي, fittingly!).
